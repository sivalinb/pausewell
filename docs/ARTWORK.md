# Illustration provenance

`assets/pausewell-story.png` is a new concept illustration derived from Siva's existing illustrated portrait, requested for this project's documentation. It does not depict measured health events. It was generated using the built-in image generation tool; the original image remains unchanged. The asset is stored in this repository rather than depending on a private cache path.

Prompt used:

> Use case: identity-preserve. Create a new wide watercolor documentation illustration for Pausewell, a non-diagnostic Apple Watch wellness check-in app. The input image is an identity and illustration-style reference: preserve the recognizable illustrated face of the adult man Siva Babu, black short hair with gray temples, medium brown skin, short stubble. Replace the RaceTime story entirely with three calm everyday scenes of this same man: at a desk wearing an Apple Watch and noticing a gentle check-in, taking a small sip of water, then stepping outdoors among trees. Warm ivory paper, deep forest green and soft teal watercolor, editorial ink details, generous whitespace. Only text: 'Pausewell' and smaller 'Notice. Check in. Choose a small pause.' No race branding, no numerical health claims, no diagnosis, no doctor imagery. This is a product concept illustration, not evidence of actual health events. Landscape 16:9 composition.

The rendered illustration includes a small decorative “A Calmer You Today” poster beyond the requested text; it is artwork, not an evaluated outcome claim.
